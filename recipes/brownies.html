Vegan Brownies Recipe

Home

Description

After months of tweaking ratios and testing every dairy-free substitute under the sun (spoiler alert: only a handful actually work), I finally landed on a recipe that my readers (and my own taste testers) now call the best vegan brownies ever.

Ingredients

  • Flax Eggs. My preferred egg sub for these brownies.
  • Black Coffee. Brewed and cooled. Instant coffee also works.
  • Chocolate Chips. Skip the expensive store-bought kind and make your own vegan chocolate chips.
  • Coconut Sugar. To sweeten the brownies and give them gorgeous crackly tops! White or brown sugar also works.
  • Vegan Butter. I tested the four most popular vegan butter substitutes on the market (Miyokos, Earth Balance, Violife, and Kite Hill) and Miyokos was the clear winner- seriously, my partner and I were fooled into thinking it wasn’t vegan.
  • Vanilla Extract. A must for any good brownie recipe!
  • All-Purpose Flour. Sifted to ensure there are no clumps.
  • Cocoa Powder. Use unsweetened and natural cocoa powder. Dutch-processed cocoa will also work, but sometimes, it can affect whether the tops become crackly.

Steps

  1. Combine the ground flaxseed with the brewed coffee. Let it sit for 10 minutes for a gel to form.
  2. Whisk the sugar and butter in a large mixing bowl until smooth. Add the vanilla extract, melted chocolate, and prepared flax eggs and whisk until combined and glossy.
  3. Mix the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
  4. Bake the brownies for 27-30 minutes, or until a skewer comes out mostly clean.

Fried Chicken Wings Recipe

Home

Description

My fried chicken wings recipe is truly crispy on the outside and has the juiciest meat in the middle. It’s SO easy to make and needs just a handful of ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Chicken wings. Choose meaty chicken wings as they will hold onto the batter better.
  • Spices. paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, cayenne pepper, and dried parsley.
  • Salt. To taste.
  • Oil. Vegetable oil, but any oil with a high smoke point works.

Steps

  1. Pat dry the chicken wings.
  2. Make the dry mixture by combining all spices with flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Toss the wings in the flour mixture until they are fully coated, and shake off the excess.

Popcorn Chicken Recipe

Home

Description

They taste better than the fast food giant and make a fantastic homemade appetizer or game-day snack. They cook in minutes and are so darn addictive. As a diehard KFC fan, I can attest these are EVEN better and so simple.

Ingredients

  • Chicken. I find chicken breasts to be easy to work with and slice into bite-sized pieces.
  • Buttermilk. Coating the chicken in buttermilk helps tenderize and infuse the meat with a mild, tangy flavor.
  • Corn flour. This super-fine flour is used to coat the chicken pieces.
  • Spices. I seasoned the corn flour with salt, smoked paprika, oregano, basil, and mustard powder.
  • Oil. I typically use canola oil, but other high-smoke-point oils, such as vegetable oil or peanut oil, work well too.

Steps

  1. Assemble. Whisk the flour and spices together in a separate bowl.
  2. Marinate. Toss and marinate the chicken pieces with the buttermilk and salt.
  3. Dredge. Drain the chicken and toss the pieces in the seasoned flour.
  4. Fry. Fry the chicken in the hot oil until it’s golden brown and crispy.
